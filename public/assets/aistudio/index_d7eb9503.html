    NOT SURE WHERE TO START?

    Follow this roadmap based on your level.

    1. Complete Beginners: 

    The very first step is Hangul. Everything builds on this, so don’t skip it.

    ​

    If you prefer self-study with books: 
    👉 Read Korean Step by Step ($7)​

    ​

    If you want guidance + video lessons:
    👉 Read Korean Like a Pro

    2. Grow Your Vocabulary:

    Lack of vocabulary will hold you back in every area of Korean. You need foundation words you can start using right away. These become the building blocks for your future sentences.​​

    ​

    👉 2,206+ Korean Words (Verbs + Nouns): Ultimate Vocabulary Power Pack 2.0

    3. Speaking & Pronunciation Help: 

    If you want to learn essential Korean phrases you can use anywhere:
    👉 Speak Korean Anywhere​​

    ​​

    If you want to practice speaking but don’t have anyone to practice with:
    👉 Shadowing Studio: shadowing practice helps you improve your tone, rhythm, pronunciation, and flow by practicing real conversations

    4. Make Sentences (Present, Past, Future)

    You know how to read Korean and have some basic vocabulary. Now it’s time to start making your own sentences.

    ​

    👉 Sentence Freedom: The Complete Korean Sentence-Making Course *Korean particle lessons are included in this course

    5. Expand Your Sentences 

    You can make sentences, but they feel a bit limited. Then let’s expand what you can say by learning essential patterns. 

    ​

    👉 The Game Changer for Korean Grammar: Learn 331 grammar patterns with done-for-you pattern flashcards, 2,000+ example sentences, and step-by-step video lessons.

    6. Going to Korea? 

    If you're planning a trip and want to feel prepared:

    ​

    👉 All-in-One Korea Travel Guide: I included my personal recommendations for restaurants, cafes, hotels, and more!

    ​

    👉 Speak Korean Anywhere: Useful phrases you can use while traveling in Korea.
